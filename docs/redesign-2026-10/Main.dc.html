How it works

01 · STYLE

What kind of player is he?

Every action per 90 minutes, compared with players in the same position, each judged against his own league.

02 · FINISHING

Are his goals real?

Each shot gets a chance of scoring from where and how it was taken. Goals above that are finishing, or luck.

03 · PRICE

Who plays like him, for less?

The closest statistical matches in any league, next to their Transfermarkt value.
